Put your own wallpaper pictures (.jpg .png .webp, ideally 1920x1080 or larger) in this folder on GitHub.
After the next build they appear in Glow under Wallpaper > My photo: <name>.
With two or more photos you also get "Rotate through all my photos". Photos drift slowly and dissolve into each other.
Only use pictures you have the right to use (your own, or free-licence sites such as Unsplash or Pexels).
